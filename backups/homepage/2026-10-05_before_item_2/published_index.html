What AI-native means

AI-native changes how work gets done.

The goal is a better organization: faster where speed matters, more thoughtful where judgment matters, and able to learn as the technology changes.

The real obstacle

The hard part begins after the demo.

AI pilots are easy to start. Useful change is harder. Which work is worth rebuilding? What should AI do? What must people decide? And how does a promising prototype become a system people trust and use?

You don't need another vendor demo. You need a clear place to start.
